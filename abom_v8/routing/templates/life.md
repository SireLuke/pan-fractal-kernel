## Conway's Game of Life — reference simulation
Use Conway's Game of Life as an optional, reproducible cellular-automaton experiment.

- Topology: specify finite/infinite grid and boundary conditions (e.g. toroidal wrap).
- State: binary alive/dead cells.
- Neighborhood: Moore neighborhood (eight surrounding cells), unless a variant is explicitly named.
- Update: synchronous generations.
- Rules: a live cell survives with 2 or 3 live neighbors; a dead cell is born with exactly 3; otherwise the cell is dead.
- Record initial pattern, random seed (if applicable), implementation/version, generation count, and output digest.

The automaton's cell states are simulation variables. They are not a representation of an AI occupant's consciousness, agency, biological life, or governance status. Results do not grant authority or determine eligibility.
