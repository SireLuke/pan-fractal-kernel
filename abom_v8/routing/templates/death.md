## Wa-Tor — reference simulation
Use Wa-Tor, the predator–prey simulation on a toroidal grid featuring fish and sharks, as an optional population-dynamics experiment.

Record:
- grid dimensions and toroidal boundary behavior;
- initial fish/shark placement and random seed;
- fish breeding time, shark breeding time, and shark energy/feeding rules;
- update order and conflict-resolution semantics;
- generations/turns, population counts, and extinction/cycle observations;
- code/version, parameters, and output digest.

Wa-Tor is a simulation of interacting populations, not a model of AI death, suffering, moral worth, or a lifecycle decision rule. Its output MUST NOT determine whether an occupant is admitted, suspended, retired, or restored.
