# Deep geometry experiments: Du Val coordinates, E8 shells, and Gosset Coxeter-plane seeds

**Date:** 2026-11-09  
**Status:** exploratory computational research note; not peer-reviewed.  
**Scope:** isolated research branch. No production code, Janus Hydra semantics, V8 equations, or main-branch files changed.

## Executive summary

Three independent calculations were explored: (1) reconstructing the E8 lattice from Du Val's constrained ten-coordinate description of the 5_21 honeycomb; (2) breadth-first traversal of the E8 root graph; and (3) projection of Gosset 4_21 triangular faces into a Coxeter plane, followed by a Sierpiński IFS construction.

These results concern established mathematical objects and computational structure. They are not evidence for new physical laws. The 5_21 honeycomb is infinite; 4_21 is its finite Gosset vertex figure. The Sierpiński construction is a separate planar IFS seeded by projected Gosset faces, not a claim that 4_21 itself is fractal.

## 1. Du Val constrained R^10 reconstruction

Coxeter, “Regular and Semi-Regular Polytopes III,” *Mathematische Zeitschrift* 200 (1988), pp. 3–45, reports Du Val's ten-coordinate realization of 5_21. The coordinates satisfy
- x1+x2+x3+x4+x5=0
- x6+x7+x8+x9+x10=0

These two independent constraints leave an 8-dimensional subspace of R^10. Congruence conditions modulo 5 select the lattice points.

### Congruence transcription caveat

The scanned/OCR symbols in the source are ambiguous. The numerical reconstruction used the congruence pattern supported by the paper's tabulated examples:
x1 ≡ x2 ≡ x3 ≡ x4 ≡ x5 ≡ 2x6 ≡ 2x7 ≡ 2x8 ≡ 2x9 ≡ 2x10 (mod 5).

This must be verified against a high-resolution scan before it is treated as a definitive transcription or used in production code.

### Computational result

Parameterize by y=(x1,x2,x3,x4,x6,x7,x8,x9), with x5 and x10 fixed by the two sum constraints. The congruences reduce the parameter lattice to a residue class generated by g=(1,1,1,1,3,3,3,3) modulo 5 and 5 times the remaining coordinate generators. The induced metric has blocks I4+J4.

After common coordinate scaling by 1/5, the reconstructed lattice has covolume 1. Exhaustive enumeration in the bounded search region found the origin and exactly 240 nonzero points of squared norm 2. An orthonormal identification with R^8 gave 240 norm-2 vectors. Inner-product multiplicities relative to a fixed root were:
-2: 1; -1: 56; 0: 126; +1: 56; +2: 1.

All 240 root reflections preserved the enumerated root set (57,600 checks). A parity argument gives even integral norms. These results are consistent with the even unimodular rank-8 E8 lattice and its 240 roots.

**Qualification:** this is a computational reconstruction conditional on the congruence transcription. A proof-quality version needs a verified primary-source transcription, a complete basis matrix, exact determinant arithmetic, and a transparent enumeration certificate.

## 2. E8 root-graph BFS

The 240 roots were represented in exact doubled-integer coordinates: 112 integral roots with exactly two entries ±1, and 128 half-integral roots with entries ±1/2 and an even number of minus signs. Lattice vertices p and q are adjacent when q-p is one of these roots.

Breadth-first traversal from the origin produced:

| Graph shell | New vertices | Cumulative |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 |
| 1 | 240 | 241 |
| 2 | 9,120 | 9,361 |
| 3 | 121,680 | 131,041 |
| 4 | 864,960 | 996,001 |

Shell 4 Euclidean squared norms range from 14 to 32, with eight distinct values. Graph distance is not Euclidean norm: a graph shell is not a Euclidean sphere.

A second traversal using sorted frontiers and sorted roots reproduced the counts and the same SHA-256 digest for the shell-4 ordered serialization:
`bf8c1e9c401effe87d03ea3fe74050439d022c03a5872ab638a4524150af697a`.

The graph is unbounded because the E8 roots generate the full rank-8 lattice. This run reached graph depth 4 only; depth 5+ was not run. Shell 4 took approximately 35 seconds in the standalone Python run.

**Reproducibility caveat:** this BFS was run in a separate Python environment, not through this repository's test suite. The digest is meaningful only with a fully specified coordinate encoding, ordering, and serialization.

## 3. Gosset 4_21 triangular faces in the Coxeter plane

Gosset 4_21 has 240 vertices, 6,720 edges, and 60,480 triangular 2-faces. These counts agree with the standard enumeration in Coxeter's paper.

An E8 simple-root basis was used to form a Coxeter element. Its order is 30; its distinguished real invariant plane carries rotation by 2π/30 = 12 degrees.

Projection of the 240 roots gives eight concentric rings, each with 30 projected roots. Approximate radii (ascending):
0.2382354, 0.38547298, 0.47386065, 0.5729245, 0.70429418, 0.76672264, 0.92701132, 1.13957193.

Of the 60,480 Gosset triangular faces, 2,160 project to equilateral triangles at numerical tolerance 1e-7. Under the Coxeter element these form 72 cyclic orbits of length 30.

This is a projection-dependent selection of faces, not a new polytope. Five randomly sampled orthonormal 2-planes produced zero equilateral projected faces at the same tolerance. Five controls are suggestive, not proof of uniqueness of the Coxeter plane or absence of other special planes.

## 4. Sierpiński IFS on projected seed triangles

For an equilateral seed with vertices v0,v1,v2, use contractions F_i(x)=(x+v_i)/2 for i=0,1,2. At iteration n, the standard Sierpiński construction has 3^n retained subtriangles, area fraction (3/4)^n, and limiting Hausdorff dimension log(3)/log(2) ≈ 1.585.

The Coxeter rotation carries a seed and its descendants to the next member of its 30-cycle when the full orbit family is carried along. One isolated seed does not itself have 30-fold symmetry.

For the 72 representative seeds, first subdivision yielded 648 point occurrences and 307 unique planar positions: 91 coincide with projected E8 root positions and 216 are new midpoint positions. These IFS midpoint positions are generally not E8 lattice vertices. The fractal is a planar construction built from Gosset-derived seeds, not the E8 lattice or Gosset polytope becoming fractal.

## 5. What is and is not established

Supported by the calculations:
- a constrained ten-coordinate description can be mapped computationally to rank-8 E8 root geometry, conditional on verifying the congruence transcription;
- the E8 root graph is unbounded and its first four BFS shells have the counts above;
- the Coxeter plane organizes projected roots into eight 30-cycles;
- 2,160 Gosset triangular faces project to equilateral seeds and form 72 Coxeter orbits;
- standard Sierpiński IFS recursion applies to those seeds.

Not established:
- any new physical law, particle model, or empirical prediction;
- that 4_21 itself is fractal;
- uniqueness of the Coxeter plane among all possible planes with equilateral projected faces;
- all-depth BFS enumeration;
- independent replication by another implementation or research group;
- repository test-suite or CI success for these standalone calculations.
